Why TechTown

Ambition needs a place to start.

The Kingdom's goals ask for more than technology. They ask for people who can build it, and a place where they can begin. TechTown exists to give talent and founders that place: practical, open, and rooted in the community around them.

Talent, not just training

Skills that map to real work. We teach the way teams actually build, so what you learn on day one holds up on the job.

Founders, not just ideas

Support from first prototype to first customers. Mentorship, workspace, and introductions that move an idea toward a working business.

Community, not just space

A network that opens doors. You grow next to people building the same future, and the connections you make here stay with you.

How to join

Four steps from interested to building.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Share who you are and what you want to work toward. It takes a few minutes.

  2. 02

    Get matched

  3. 03

    Start building

Vision 2030

Part of the Kingdom's digital ambition.

Vision 2030 sets a clear goal: a diversified economy built on Saudi skills and Saudi enterprise. TechTown backs that goal where it is most practical, by growing the people who will build the digital economy and the companies that will run in it.

  • Building national capability in software, data, and AI
  • Helping founders start and grow companies at home
  • Widening the path into tech for young Saudis

Every program, cohort, and partnership is measured against one question: does it help Saudis build?
