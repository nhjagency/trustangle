Why techtawn

Ambition needs a place to start.

Syria's future asks for more than technology. It asks for people who can build it, and a place where they can begin. techtawn exists to give talent and founders that place: practical, open, and rooted in the community around them.

Talent, not just training

Skills that map to real work. We teach the way teams actually build, so what you learn on day one holds up on the job.

Founders, not just ideas

Support from first prototype to first customers. Mentorship, workspace, and introductions that move an idea toward a working business.

Community, not just space

A network that opens doors. You grow next to people building the same future, and the connections you make here stay with you.

Our mission

A new chapter for Syrian tech.

Syria has a young, capable generation and a real chance to rebuild its economy on digital foundations. techtawn backs that where it is most practical: growing the people who will build the digital economy, helping founders start companies at home, and connecting Syrian talent to opportunity both inside the country and beyond it.

  • Building local capability in software, data, and design
  • Helping founders start and grow companies at home
  • Connecting Syrian talent to work at home and abroad

Every program, cohort, and partnership is measured against one question: does it help Syrians build?
